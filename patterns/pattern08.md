## Pattern `08`: Overclock

The arrangement reaches its densest reusable form without dropping out or restarting. The first quarter drives the sax through an ascending `D-F-G-A` figure; the second gives Ch4 to a quicker MuteClav exchange and a low `Tubes` pivot. The second half restates the sax climb at greater intensity and reaches `B-4` before falling to `A-4`. A final `A-3` Tubes strike and A bass pickup leave a dominant charge that resolves cleanly into either Pattern `06` or Pattern `04`.
